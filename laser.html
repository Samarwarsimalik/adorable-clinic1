Laser hair reduction: what determines the plan?

Hair thickness, colour, treatment area, skin tone, growth cycle and previous hair-removal methods influence the number and spacing of sessions.

Common treatment areas

  • Face, chin and beard shaping
  • Underarms and arms
  • Chest and back
  • Legs
  • Full-body plans

Why multiple sessions are common

Hair grows in cycles. Laser is most effective on follicles in an active growth phase, so treatment is usually spaced across multiple visits rather than performed once.
