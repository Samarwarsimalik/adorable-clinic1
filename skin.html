What to expect from a skin consultation

Your clinician may review the main concern, duration, current products, previous treatments, medical history and sensitivity before recommending an approach.

Why diagnosis matters

Acne, melasma, post-inflammatory pigmentation, tanning and irritation can look similar at a glance but may need very different treatment choices. Aggressive treatment without assessment can worsen some concerns.

A practical skin-care sequence

  • Identify the concern and likely triggers.
  • Stabilise active irritation or acne when necessary.
  • Select procedures only when the skin is suitable.
  • Use sun protection and aftercare consistently.
  • Review progress rather than changing plans too quickly.
