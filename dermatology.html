Dermatology

When a skin problem needs assessment before aesthetics.

Persistent rashes, dermatitis, fungal concerns, oily scalp and changing lesions should be evaluated clinically rather than treated as a cosmetic issue first.

Book consultation ↗
Clinical concerns

Assessment comes before procedure.

For dermatitis, fungal infections, oily skin/scalp and other dermatology concerns, the principle is simple: assess first, then treat.

01

Dermatitis & irritation

Rashes and inflamed skin can have different triggers, so treatment should follow assessment.

02

Fungal concerns

Proper diagnosis matters because not every itchy or scaly rash is fungal.

03

Scalp concerns

Oily scalp, flaking and irritation may need a clinical plan before hair procedures.

04

Pigment changes

Melasma, post-inflammatory marks and other pigmentation can behave differently.

05

Lesions

Moles, warts or changing spots should be assessed before any removal procedure is considered.

06

Acne

Moderate or persistent acne may need a broader medical approach, not just a facial or peel.
